# Decisions

Two parts. **Open**: questions you are carrying around but have not decided. **Log**: what you decided, newest on top. When an open question is decided, move it to the log with the date and the reason.

The reason matters more than the decision: it is what lets you revisit it later without starting over. Direction (what you want, what you never do) lives in `compass.md`; this file is where that direction turns into choices.
