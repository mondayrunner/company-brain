# Compass

What you want, and what you do not. Your agent reads this before it advises you, so its advice points your way instead of towards a generic "grow the business".

**Everything here is optional.** Fill in what you know now and leave the rest empty. A half-filled compass still works; the agent says what it is missing. The structure follows Dan Koe's components: an anti-vision and a boundary let an adviser notice things you never wrote a goal for.

Who you are and what you sell does not belong here. That lives in `company.md`, `positioning.md` and `pricing.md`.

---

## Anti-vision

The company and the life you do not want. Be unkind to yourself here; this section does the most work. Not "I don't want a boring company", but: what does a Tuesday look like five years from now if nothing changes, and what exactly is wrong with it?
