# Template 2 — Intake

As soon as the date is fixed. The intake can be a form, a short call, or an AI chat interview. Deadline about a week before the day, so you can read everything and plan.

---

To: [ALL PARTICIPANTS]
Subject: Workshop day [DATE]: address and a short intake

Hi [NAMES],

On [DAY DATE] we build together at [ADDRESS], from 9:30 to about 16:30.

What we build exactly depends on where your time goes now. So before the day I ask each of you a few questions:

[LINK OR HOW THE INTAKE WORKS]

It takes about ten minutes. Only I read the answers.

Could you do it by [DEADLINE]? Then I read everything before the day. The preparation list follows [WEEK BEFORE].

Best,
[YOU]
